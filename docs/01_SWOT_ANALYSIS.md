# SWOT Analysis

## Strengths
- High compatibility with various devices.
- Strong community support leading to regular updates.

## Weaknesses
- Occasional driver stability issues across different operating systems.
- Limited feature set compared to competitors.

## Opportunities
- Expanding market for wireless technology.
- Potential partnerships with hardware manufacturers.

## Threats
- Increasing competition in the wireless adapter market.
- Rapid technology advancements outpacing current capabilities.
